Software for distributors of medicines and supplements: stock, pharmacies, managers

Stock by batch and expiry, deliveries to pharmacies and their debts, visits, managers' bonuses and the month's result - in one database, on your own computers, without the internet.

Windows · macOS · Android · iOS

Three places where the money sits

Some of the money is with managers who carry the goods. Some is in pharmacies that have not paid yet. The rest is on the shelf. Each place is counted somehow on its own; together - nowhere.

  • With managers

    How much stock is in their hands, where they were today, what they recorded and how much they earned themselves.

  • In pharmacies

    What was delivered on consignment, what was sold, what came back and how much the pharmacy still owes.

  • In stock

    Quantities by batch, expiry dates and the price the company paid for the goods.

Nothing is stored ready-made. Debt, stock on hand and the month's result are computed from the records every time - so they cannot be “forgotten to update”.

Five questions that usually have no answer by the evening

  1. Who owes us, and how much - right now?

    Pharmacies that have not paid for what was delivered - by age: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+. The company's debt is visible only where every manager's records sit together.

    See the details

  2. Whom do we owe?

    The month closed, the manager's bonus was accrued - and not paid. Debt and “to pay” are one number with different signs, and both must be shown.

    See the details

  3. How much did we really earn this month?

    Sales minus the cost of what was sold, minus the accrued bonuses. Accrued, not paid.

    See the details

  4. Was the manager at the pharmacy?

    By plan and across everyone, not once at three convenient ones. A manager's report is not proof of arrival.

    See the details

  5. What expires in 90 days?

    There is an answer only where every box has a batch and a date. “600 units in total” is not an answer.

    See the details

How it works, in short

  • No connection - work does not stop

    Every record is made on the device itself - in the office and in the field alike. A connection is needed to exchange data, and nowhere else.

  • Sync

    When there is a connection - by itself; when you need it now - by a button. The data stays yours: the database is encrypted, the backup is a file.

  • What it runs on

    Windows and macOS - the office. Android and iOS - the field.
