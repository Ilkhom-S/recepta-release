Features

One section per module - in the order a distributor asks about them.

Stock

ready

Goods received in batches with a price and an expiry date; quantities by batch and location - in the warehouse, with managers, in pharmacies.

  • Stock on hand is the sum of movements, not a figure someone corrected
  • An expiry date on every batch answers “what expires in 90 days”
  • The purchase price is known for every batch; the value on hand is on the screen
  • A goods receipt is posted once; a mistake is fixed by a correction, not an edit

Pharmacies

ready

Every pharmacy has its card: deliveries, sales reports, returns, payments and the account - all in one place.

  • Delivery on consignment and sale are different events
  • What it was delivered for and at what discount is visible on the record
  • A return is its own operation, with a reason
  • The pharmacy's debt by age: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90 days
  • A reconciliation statement per pharmacy prints and adds up

Visits and routes

ready

You set where to go and how often; the application compares the plan with the visits and shows coverage and frequency per manager.

  • A visit plan and the order of the day's round
  • The phone notices that the manager has arrived and offers to record the visit
  • A manager's report is not proof of arrival; these are two different events
  • Visit discipline is its own report: coverage, frequency, cancellations

Managers and their bonuses

ready

The application computes the bonus by your scheme and shows line by line what it is made of.

  • The scheme is a list of ready rules with switches
  • Switch one on, off, or change a number - and from this month it counts that way
  • A percentage of turnover, steps for meeting the plan, a supplement for focus products
  • Pharmacy debt that does not close reduces the bonus as a separate, plain line
  • A closed month fixes the calculation; the director's adjustment is a separate line with a reason

Reports and the month's result

ready

Every table exports to Excel and PDF. The month's result - sales minus cost of goods, minus accrued bonuses - is one sheet.

  • Overview - one screen for all managers
  • Pharmacy debt by age
  • Visit discipline
  • The month's result - after bonuses, not before; accrued, not paid
  • Cost belongs to the batch
  • Checks: a product without a price, a possible duplicate, an open period
  • Printed documents are always black on white

Sync

in development

The office computer and the managers' phones work on one database: the manager records on their device, the director checks on theirs.

  • Every device records on itself; a connection is needed only to exchange
  • When there is a connection - by itself; when you need it now - by a button
  • Users and roles: a login for every employee, the administrator decides who sees what
  • The server belongs to the vendor or to you

Security and backups

ready

The database is encrypted, sign-in is by PIN and fingerprint, the money ledger is never rewritten, the backup is a file that opens on another computer.

  • Every action is recorded with the name of who did it
  • The backup is an encrypted file; the vendor holds no key to it
  • The application reminds you when the backup is older than the chosen age
  • Restore and export are always available, regardless of the licence

Feedback

in development

“Write to us” is in the settings and on every error message; the reply arrives in the same thread.

  • An error, a suggestion or a question - three buttons, one field
  • Technical details are attached only by choice - without names, phones or amounts
  • A screenshot is optional, and the application warns that the register may be on it
  • What was sent is a list with a status: waiting to send, sent, answered

In development

in development

What is being built now and comes next.

  • Sync - a server, the managers' phones, several devices
  • Notifications - about expiry dates, debts and an open month
  • Feedback - the reply to “Write to us” arrives in the same thread
  • App stores - Microsoft Store, Google Play and the App Store
