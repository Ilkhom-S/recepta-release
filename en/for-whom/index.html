Who it is for

One application, but everyone in the company needs it for their own reason.

The owner and the director

Where the company's money is right now and how much of it came back. The real result of the month - after the cost of goods and the accrued bonuses are subtracted, not before. Which pharmacies hold debt and for how long. And, above all, what the company cannot explain: a product without a price, a batch without an expiry date, a delivery without a sales report - named on the screen, not hidden in a zero.

The question “across everyone” is asked once. There is no need to assemble the answer from managers' separate files - the answer is already shared, because the records are shared.

The manager

The plan for the day and the route - in the morning, not from memory in the evening. A bonus they can check line by line, instead of a sum to be taken on trust. A delivery and a sales report recorded right at the pharmacy. And noticeably less evening work: the record is made where they stand, and the phone needs no internet.

Honest both ways. The manager sees their own trail exactly as the director does. There is no hidden tracking in the product - otherwise the phone would simply be switched off.

The office and accounting

Documents that print and reconcile: a delivery note, a reconciliation statement per pharmacy, a bonus sheet. Export to Excel and PDF from every report screen - not “export everything” but exactly the list on the screen. And a month that can be closed and stays closed: a correction is a dated adjustment in the next period, not a quiet backdated edit.

An open period is honestly marked. While the month is open, every export page says the figures are preliminary.

Company size

From two to fifty representatives. Distributors of medicines and supplements. Installed in one day, without consultants.

Not for a multinational that already has a cloud CRM and an implementation department.
