Privacy policy

In short: the site collects nothing about visitors - no analytics, no tracking, one cookie for the chosen language; the data in the application belongs to the customer, and the customer is the controller of personal data.

Draft - to be reviewed by counsel. The text describes how the application and the site work; the wording is not final until counsel has reviewed it.

Who we are

The vendor of Recepta is ООО «Организация», г. Душанбе, ул. Примерная 1. You can reach us at hello@recepta.example and by the other means on the Contact page.

This policy covers two different things: this site and the Recepta application. They work differently, and each is described on its own.

This site

The site collects no data about visitors: no analytics, no tracking. One cookie to remember the chosen language (recepta_lang, the language code and nothing else, kept for a year); it is set only when you pick a language yourself and is sent nowhere. There are no third-party scripts or fonts: the site loads nothing from anyone else's servers.

The site is hosted by a hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.). Like any server, it sees the IP address and the requested page in its own logs under its own policy. We do not receive those logs.

The only things that leave the site are what you send yourself: a click to the releases page or to a messenger, and a message from the “Write to us” form if you submit one. A sent message - the contact you gave and the text - is stored on the vendor's server to answer you, for as long as the answer takes, and is deleted on request.

The Recepta application

The application runs on the customer's own computers and phones. Records about pharmacies, employees, deliveries, payments and visits are the customer's data; the customer is the controller of personal data and processes them under the law of the country where they operate. The application was designed against the Republic of Tajikistan's Law No. 1537 “On the protection of personal data” (2018).

In the “Single device” edition the vendor holds no copy of the customer's data at all: the database sits on the customer's computer and is encrypted.

The customer makes backups themselves, to a file; the vendor holds no key to that backup in any edition. The vendor cannot restore a backup without the customer's password.

Updates

On Windows and macOS the application checks a public address for a new version. The request carries no identifier - not of the computer, the customer or the licence. The check can be switched off; an update is never required and never happens silently.

“Write to us”

The application has a “Write to us” button. We receive exactly what the customer chose to send, and the customer sees the whole message before sending. Technical details - which screen, which version - are attached only by choice, and names, phones and amounts are removed from them before they are shown.

A message is kept as long as the conversation needs it and deleted on request.

The “Sync” edition

When the “Sync” edition ships, this policy gains a section on the server: where it is located, who owns it and on what terms it processes the customer's data (the processing agreement). Customers will be told in advance.

Your rights and how to reach us

A visitor of the site gives us nothing, so there is nothing to delete; the language cookie is removed in the browser's settings. For data in the application the customer turns to their own administrator - the data is with them, not with us. Questions to the vendor - on the Contact page.
